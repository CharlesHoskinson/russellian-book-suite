# iacr-review

EC22-grounded IACR/EUROCRYPT review rubric for cryptography papers. Dispatches ten
expert-persona reviewers (game-based crypto, UC, concrete security, BFT consensus,
P2P networks, TEEs, formal methods, applied crypto, post-quantum, economics/MEV)
in parallel, each filling sections A through I of the EUROCRYPT 2022 review form,
then consolidates findings into a single ledger sorted by severity.

The rubric is harsh by default: IACR top-tier acceptance rates hover near 22%, so
the personas lean toward weak/strong reject unless the paper clearly improves on
prior state of the art.

See `SKILL.md` for the operating doctrine, persona dispatch protocol, and ledger
format. The verbatim EC22 form lives at `personas/ec22-rubric.md`.
